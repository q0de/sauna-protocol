Safety Warning

Extreme heat can be dangerous. Do not attempt this protocol if you have a history of heart conditions, low blood pressure, or are pregnant. Always hydrate with electrolytes before and after. If you feel dizzy, exit immediately.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I do this? expand_more
The Blueprint protocol recommends 4-7 sessions per week for maximum longevity benefits. However, starting with 2-3 sessions is advised for beginners.
Is Infrared better than Traditional? expand_more
Bryan uses a high-heat traditional sauna (dry heat). Infrared typically tops out at 140-150°F, which may not trigger the same level of Heat Shock Proteins as 200°F dry heat, though it has other benefits.
Can I eat before the session? expand_more
It is recommended to wait at least 1-2 hours after a heavy meal. Blood flow is diverted to the skin for cooling during sauna use, which can interfere with digestion.
